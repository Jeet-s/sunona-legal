Privacy Policy — Suno Na

Last updated: October 11, 2026

Suno Na ("Suno Na", "we", "us") is an AI companion app operated by

Saath, Delhi, India. This policy explains what we collect, why,

who we share it with, and the choices you have. By using Suno Na you agree to this

policy. Suno Na is for adults 18 and older only.

1. Who this applies to

Everyone who creates an account or uses Suno Na on any platform (Android, iOS, web).

2. Information we collect

You give us:

  • Account — when you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email address,

and Google account identifier.

  • Profile you set up — the name/nickname you want to be called, your language

preference, goals, boundaries, and personality preferences for your companion.

  • Your conversations — the text messages, voice notes, and photos you send in

the app, and the companion's replies.

  • Purchases — records of credit bundles you buy (handled by the app stores /

RevenueCat; we do not receive your card number).

Collected automatically:

  • Usage & device data — app interactions, credit activity, crash/diagnostic

data, device type and OS version, and a push-notification token (if you allow

notifications).

  • Derived "memories" — to make the companion feel continuous, we store short

facts and preferences inferred from your conversations. You can view and delete

these in the app (Memories / Trust & Privacy).

We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. See Section 8.

3. How we use your information

  • To provide the core experience: generate replies, voice, and images, remember

context, and personalize your companion.

  • To operate credits and purchases.
  • To send notifications you've opted into (gentle check-ins; you can turn these

off in Settings and in your OS).

  • To keep the service safe (e.g. detecting crisis or self-reported-minor signals)

and to prevent abuse and fraud.

  • To fix bugs, measure product performance, and improve the app.
  • To comply with law.

4. AI processing & who we share data with (subprocessors)

Suno Na uses trusted third-party providers to deliver features. Your content is

sent to them only to provide the service:

  • Supabase — database, authentication, storage, and serverless functions (data

hosting).

  • Anthropic (Claude) — generating the companion's text replies.
  • ElevenLabs — text-to-speech (the companion's voice) and speech-to-text

(transcribing your voice notes).

  • OpenAI — generating companion photos ("selfies") on request (if enabled).
  • RevenueCat and the Apple App Store / Google Play — processing purchases.
  • PostHog — product analytics (if enabled).
  • Expo — push notification delivery.

We do not sell your personal information. We do not use your private

conversations to train third-party foundation models beyond what is necessary to

generate your replies, and we ask our providers not to train on your content where

that option is available.

5. Storage, security & international transfers

Data is stored on Supabase infrastructure and may be processed on servers outside

India by the providers listed above. We use encryption in transit, access

controls, and row-level security so each user can only access their own data.

No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your account and conversation data while your account is active. When you

delete your account (Settings → Trust & Privacy → Delete account), we delete your

profile, messages, memories, generated media, and stored audio, and remove your

authentication record. Some records may persist briefly in backups or where the

law requires retention.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Access / export — export your data from Settings → Trust & Privacy.
  • Edit / delete memories — manage what the companion remembers in-app.
  • Delete your account and data — in Settings, at any time.
  • Notifications — opt out in Settings or your OS.

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable

laws, you may have rights to access, correct, and erase your data, to withdraw

consent, and to grievance redressal. To exercise these, contact us at

saathstudio26@gmail.com. Our Grievance Officer can be reached at the same

address: saathstudio26@gmail.com.

8. Age limits

Suno Na is strictly for users 18 and older. We do not knowingly allow minors to

use the app; if we learn a user is under 18 we will restrict and delete the

account. If you believe a minor is using Suno Na, contact us at

saathstudio26@gmail.com.

9. Not a crisis or medical service

Suno Na is an AI companion for entertainment and emotional support. It is not a

therapist, medical provider, or emergency service, and does not give professional

advice. If you are in distress, contact a qualified professional or a helpline

(in India: KIRAN 1800-599-0019, iCall 9152987821).

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; we'll post the new version here and update the date

above, and notify you of material changes in-app.

11. Contact

Saath — saathstudio26@gmail.com — Delhi, India.
